About me

Finance training and machine learning practice, applied to the same set of problems.

I study Financial Technology at Bahrain Polytechnic and completed the full-time Data Science programme run by General Assembly at BIBF. That gives me both halves of the problem: investment analysis, corporate finance and information systems on one side; Python, SQL, statistics and machine learning on the other.

The combination shapes how I work. A churn model isn't finished when the ROC-AUC stops improving — it's finished when someone in retention knows who to call this week and what it costs to be wrong. I spend as much time on framing the question and weighing the trade-offs as on tuning the model, and I'd rather report an honest result than a flattering one.

A rotational apprenticeship at Rain Management showed me how a licensed digital-asset platform actually runs day to day, across compliance, operations and corporate structure. I'm looking for a data science or analytics role where financial context is an advantage — ideally one working on financial inclusion and personalised digital finance in the Gulf.

Capstone project

Bank customer churn prediction

A classification model that flags retail-banking customers at risk of leaving, built on 28,382 accounts with an 18.53% churn rate. I compared logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost using GridSearchCV and class weighting, and engineered a feature that ended up driving 39% of the final model's predictions.

0.84ROC-AUC, with 0.70 recall on churners

  • Pandas

Kaggle competition

Bahrain property rent prediction

An end-to-end regression pipeline predicting monthly rent across 10,578 Bahraini listings. The hard part was the data, not the model: numeric fields stored as text, and a target spanning 50 to 400,000 BHD that needed a log transform. I engineered high-cardinality location and agency features and tuned a Ridge model with GridSearchCV.

0.77test R², with MAE of 100 BHD
